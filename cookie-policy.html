What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit, which can make your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

Cookies we use

This website uses only analytics cookies provided by Google Analytics (Google LLC). We do not use advertising, tracking, or any third-party marketing cookies.

Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_ga Google Analytics Distinguishes unique users 2 years
_ga_* Google Analytics Maintains session state 2 years

Local storage

We also use your browser's localStorage (not a cookie) to remember your cookie consent choice and to store high scores for the arcade games on this site. This data never leaves your device.
