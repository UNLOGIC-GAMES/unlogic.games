3. What types of cookies do we use?

This website uses the following categories of cookies:

a) Technical or strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are necessary for the basic functioning of the website and to enable navigation and use of its functionalities. These cookies allow, for example:

  • management of website security;
  • storage of technical preferences;
  • enabling essential functionalities;
  • maintenance of user sessions;
  • or balancing network traffic.

Technical cookies may involve the processing of certain personal data or technical identifiers, such as IP addresses or session identifiers. These cookies are strictly necessary to enable communication between the user's device and the network or to provide a service expressly requested by the user.

b) Analytics or measurement cookies

We use Google Analytics to obtain aggregated statistical information regarding how users interact with the website. These cookies allow us to:

  • analyse website traffic and usage;
  • identify the number of visitors;
  • determine the most visited pages;
  • improve content and functionalities;
  • and optimise the user experience.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • partially anonymised IP addresses;
  • online identifiers;
  • browser and device information;
  • visited pages;
  • duration of visits;
  • and interactions carried out on the website.

These cookies will only be installed once you have accepted their use through the cookie banner or cookie settings panel.

6. How can you manage cookies?

You can accept, reject or configure the use of cookies through the cookie banner or settings panel available on the website. You may also allow, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Below you can find information on how to manage cookies in the main browsers:

Google Chrome

Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data

Chrome (desktop) – Manage cookies

Mozilla Firefox

Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

Firefox – Information about cookies

Safari (macOS)

Safari → Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Safari macOS – Manage cookies

Safari (iPhone and iPad)

Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data

Safari iPhone/iPad – Manage cookies

Microsoft Edge

Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data

Microsoft Edge – Manage cookies

Opera

Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and other site data

Opera – Cookie management

Brave

Settings → Shields → Cookies and other site data

Brave – Cookie management

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the proper functioning of the website.
