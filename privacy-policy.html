10. Your data protection rights

You may exercise your data protection rights by sending a request to privacy@unlogic.games

  • Access: to know what personal data we process about you and obtain information about how we use it.
  • Rectification: to request the amendment or update of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: to request the deletion of your personal data when, among other reasons, it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Objection: to object to certain processing activities based on legitimate interest or to the sending of marketing communications.
  • Restriction of processing: to request that we limit the use of your personal data in certain legally circumstances provided for by law.
  • Portability: to receive your personal data in a structured format and transmit it to another controller where technically feasible.
  • Withdrawal of consent: to withdraw the consent you have given at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of the processing carried out before such withdrawal.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority; in Spain, this is the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
